# Actor-style lines bundle (AS001–AS030)

Original lines written for your channel, in the *comedy style* of famous Kannada comedians.
They are not copied dialogues, and you record them in **your own voice**: you play the
character type, you don't impersonate the actor. Safe for long videos and Shorts.

SFX names match your soundboard (`01_Meme_SFX/…`). Record each line 3 ways
(normal / over-acting / deadpan) and keep the best.

## Tips
- Don't say "I am Sadhu Kokila" or copy their famous lines word for word. You're playing a
  *type* of character.
- Use the SFX right on the punch word, not after a gap.
- Coastal lines (AS028–AS030) work best if you or a friend can do the accent naturally.
