# Recording meme audio on a phone

- **Room**: small room with curtains/bed/clothes (less echo). Fan and AC off.
- **Distance**: phone mic 10–15 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side (avoids "p/b" pops).
- **App**: any voice recorder; set highest quality (WAV or 48 kHz if available).
- **Takes**: say each line **3 times** – normal, over-acting, deadpan. The best one wins.
- **Gaps**: leave 1 second of silence before and after each line (easy to trim).
- **Energy**: meme audio needs ~30% more expression than normal talk. Exaggerate.
- **Naming**: `MOV001.wav` (movie mimicry), `CP001.wav` (catchphrase), `VO001.wav` (voiceover).
- **Batch**: 20 lines per session, drink water, take a 5-minute break.
- **Upgrade later**: a ₹300–500 clip-on (lavalier) mic makes a big difference.
