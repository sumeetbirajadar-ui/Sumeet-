\textbf{\color{crimson}5. Teaching takeaways}\par\smallskip
{\footnotesize\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em,nosep]
\item A very NCERT-based paper: definitions and one-line facts (Young's modulus, Pascal's law, SI unit of specific heat, $\frac32kT$, sign convention, discovery of the nucleus).
\item Solved Examples asked again: Example 1.2 (significant figures in density), Example 2.6 (stopping distance $\propto v^2$), Example 1.4 (touching charged spheres), Example 4.1 (wire held up by a magnetic field), Example 7.5 (iron rod in the coil dims the bulb), Example 9.3 (concave mirror, $R=15$ cm, object at 10 cm), Example 5.11 (moderator slows neutrons).
\item Exercises repeated: Exercise 4.13 (spring balance in a freely falling lift), Exercise 5.15 (power of a pump).
\item Nine questions are on deleted portions (needle oscillation, eddy currents, dip, scattering, decay, Zener diode, transistor, logic gates, communication); KCET 2017 was set on the pre-2023 syllabus.
\end{itemize}}\par\medskip
\textbf{\color{crimson}6. Deleted portions}\par\smallskip
\textbf{9} of the 60 questions are on topics removed from the 2026-27 NCERT (rationalised syllabus). They are marked \textsc{Deleted Portion} and are not analysed: oscillation period of a magnetic needle (Q 33); eddy currents (magnetic braking) (Q 36); Earth's magnetism (angle of dip) (Q 37); scattering of light (Q 44); alpha- and beta-decay (Q 53); Zener diode (Q 56); transistor (Q 57); digital electronics (logic gates) (Q 59); communication systems (Q 60).\par
