## Units and Measurements (1 questions)

**Q20.** The vernier scale used for measurement has a positive zero error of 0.2 mm. If while taking a measurement it was noted that 0 on the vernier scale lies between 8.5 cm and 8.6 cm. Vernier coincidence is 6, then the correct value of measurement is ___ cm (least count = 0.01 cm)
