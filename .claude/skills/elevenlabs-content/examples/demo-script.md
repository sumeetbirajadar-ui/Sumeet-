## Intro
Welcome to ElevenLabs. The voice AI platform built for developers.

## The API
With one API call, you get speech in 32 languages with under 75 milliseconds latency. No infrastructure to manage. No models to train. Just text in, audio out.

## Voice Cloning
Clone any voice with a 60-second audio sample. Your users can speak in their own voice, in any language, instantly.

## Sound Effects
Need a swoosh for your app transition? A notification ping? Describe it in plain English and get production-ready audio back in seconds.
