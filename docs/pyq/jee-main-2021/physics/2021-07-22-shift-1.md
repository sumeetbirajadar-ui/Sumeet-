## Units and Measurements (1 questions)

**Q22.** Three students S1, S2 and S3 perform an experiment for determining the acceleration due to gravity (g) using a simple pendulum. They use different lengths of pendulum and record time for different number of oscillations. The observations are as shown in the table.

Student No. | Length of pendulum (cm) | Number of oscillations (n) | Total time for n oscillations (s) | Time period (s)
1 | 64.0 | 8 | 128.0 | 16.0
2 | 64.0 | 4 | 64.0 | 16.0
3 | 20.0 | 4 | 36.0 | 9.0

(Least count of length = 0.1 cm, least count for time = 0.1 s)

If E1, E2 and E3 are the percentage errors in g for students 1, 2 and 3, respectively, then the minimum percentage error is obtained by student no. ___.
**Answer:** 1
