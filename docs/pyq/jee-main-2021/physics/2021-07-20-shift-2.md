## Motion in a Straight Line (1 question)

**Q4.** A boy reaches the airport and finds that the escalator is not working. He walks up the stationary escalator in time t1. If he remains stationary on a moving escalator then the escalator takes him up in time t2. The time taken by him to walk up on the moving escalator will be:
