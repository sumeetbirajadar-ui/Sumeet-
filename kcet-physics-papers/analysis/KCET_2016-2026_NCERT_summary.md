# KCET Physics 2016–2026: NCERT analysis summary

Every question was checked by hand against the official 2026-27 NCERT Physics textbooks (Class XI Parts 1–2 and Class XII Parts 1–2) and the official NCERT Exemplar, downloaded from ncert.nic.in. No third-party analysis was used. For applied questions, the closest earlier question was found by searching 193 downloaded papers: NEET/AIPMT/AIIMS 2005–2026, AIEEE 2005–2012, JEE Main 2013–2025 and IIT-JEE/JEE Advanced.

Notes
- "Similar earlier PYQ" counts only applied questions. Direct questions that also appeared in earlier papers are not counted.
- "Topic not in 2026-27 NCERT" lists questions on content removed when NCERT was rationalised: Doppler effect, radioactive decay, logic gates, transistor, Zener diode/LED, colour code, cyclotron, Earth's magnetism, Curie's law, hysteresis, LC oscillations, Q-factor, radiation pressure, communication systems, error analysis and similar topics. These are tagged Applied and carry a note in the book.
- Difficulty is the editor's assessment.
- Word-for-word repeats include: 2025 Q47 (JEE Main 2022), 2024 Q54 (NEET 2016 Phase II), 2022 Q25/Q28/Q44/Q46/Q52/Q53 (IIT-JEE 2010, NEET 2022, AIPMT 2008, AIPMT 2012, AIPMT 2008, AIEEE 2011), 2020 Q38/Q56 (AIPMT 2015, NEET 2017), 2019 Q49 (AIEEE 2005), 2018 Q59 (AIEEE 2012), 2017 Q29 (AIPMT 2008).
- Each year's per-question tags, pages and sources are in `data<year>.py` and in the book itself.
