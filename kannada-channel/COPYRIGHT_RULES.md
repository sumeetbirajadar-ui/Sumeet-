# Copyright rules for the channel

1. **Never** paste original film/TV audio into long videos or into these folders.
   Kannada film audio is owned by labels (Lahari, Anand Audio, PRK Audio, Saregama…) and
   YouTube Content ID flags even 3–10 second clips.
2. **Original movie sounds → only inside Shorts/Reels**, added from the in-app *Add sound*
   library (those are licensed for use inside that app only).
3. **Review / commentary videos** may show short original clips *while you talk about them*
   (fair dealing for review/criticism). Expect claims you may need to dispute.
4. **Main audio = your own**: re-recorded mimicry/parody lines, original catchphrases,
   your voiceovers.
5. **Sound effects & music** only from: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, ZapSplat, Uppbeat,
   Freesound (CC0 only), Epidemic Sound (paid, safelist your channel), or AI tools with a
   commercial licence (ElevenLabs paid plan, Suno paid plan).
6. **Voice cloning**: only your own voice, or someone who gave written consent.
