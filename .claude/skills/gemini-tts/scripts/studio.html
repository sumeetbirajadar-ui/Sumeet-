Gemini Voice Studio

Text to speech, dialogue, voice design and voice cloning with Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS. Runs on your machine; audio goes only to Google's API.

Turn text into speech

Write the words exactly as they should be spoken. Put the mood in the Style box, and click the tags to drop laughs, sighs and pauses at exact spots.

Write a scene

One line per turn. Give each line a speaker and, if you want, a style like "excited" or "whispering, tired". Use the same speaker on every line for a single narrator with changing emotions.

Cast

Pick a voice for each speaker. Your cloned and designed voices are listed first.
