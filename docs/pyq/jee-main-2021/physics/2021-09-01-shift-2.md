## Units and Measurements (1 question)

**Q1.** A student determined Young's Modulus of elasticity using the formula Y = MgL³/(4bd³δ). The value of g is taken to be 9.8 m s⁻² without any significant error, his observations are as follows.

| Physical Quantity | Least count of the Equipment used for measurement | Observed Value |
|---|---|---|
| Mass (M) | 1 g | 2 kg |
| Length of bar (L) | 1 mm | 1 m |
| Breadth of bar (b) | 0.1 mm | 4 cm |
| Thickness of bar (d) | 0.01 mm | 0.4 cm |
| Depression (δ) | 0.01 mm | 5 mm |

Then the fractional error in the measurement of Y is:
(1) 0.155 (2) 0.0083 (3) 0.083 (4) 0.0155
**Answer:** (4)
