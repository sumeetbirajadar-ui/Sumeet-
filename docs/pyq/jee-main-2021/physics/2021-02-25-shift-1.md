(1) (a)→(ii), (b)→(iii), (c)→(iv), (d)→(i) (2) (a)→(i), (b)→(ii), (c)→(iv), (d)→(iii) (3) (a)→(iii), (b)→(ii), (c)→(iv), (d)→(i) (4) (a)→(iii), (b)→(iv), (c)→(ii), (d)→(i)
