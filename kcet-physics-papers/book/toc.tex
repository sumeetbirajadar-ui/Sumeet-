\textbf{\color{navy}About this book.} Each paper has 60 multiple-choice questions, one mark each, to be attempted in 80 minutes, with no negative marking. Questions keep their original numbers and option labels, so they match the official KEA answer keys for the version named above. Every question has been retyped and every diagram redrawn from the English version of the paper. Every question was checked one by one against the four 2026-27 NCERT books (Class XI Part 1 \& 2, Class XII Part 1 \& 2) and carries a tag: \textsc{Direct} (answer stated in the textbook, an NCERT solved Example or Exercise, or a single printed formula), \textsc{Applied} (NCERT concept used in several steps or a new situation), or \textsc{Deleted Portion} (topic removed from the rationalised syllabus; not analysed). Direct and Applied tags give the NCERT page with a tappable link to that chapter's official PDF; applied questions also name the closest earlier NEET/AIPMT/AIIMS, AIEEE or JEE question. Each paper is followed by its answer key (solved by the editor, with notes where the printed options are faulty) and a one-page analysis. The book ends with an eleven-year summary, the 15 most repeated topics and a preparation plan.\\[8pt]
