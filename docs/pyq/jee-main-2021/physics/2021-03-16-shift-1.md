(1) [a rises linearly from 2 m/s² at x = 0 to 10 m/s² at x = 200 m, then continues as shown for x = 200–400 m]
(2) [a falls along a curve from 10 m/s² to 2 m/s² over x = 0–200 m, then stays near constant beyond 200 m]
