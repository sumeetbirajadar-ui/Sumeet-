*Remix → Use this sound* on a Short you like). Those in-app sounds are licensed for that app.

How to use: type the search term in the sound search → preview → save the ones you like to
your in-app *Favourites* (bookmark) so you can reuse them fast.

## General search terms (mix of everyone)
- `kannada comedy dialogue`, `kannada funny dialogue`, `kannada meme sound`
- `kannada troll sound`, `kannada comedy bgm`, `kannada reaction sound`
- `kannada mass dialogue` (for fake hero entries), `kannada sad bgm` (for fake-emotional bits)
- Add a film name: `<film name> comedy scene`

## Tips
- Sounds that show a **big number of Shorts** using them are trending: use those early.
- Keep a note in your phone of which sound fits which **emotion tag** (same tags as your bundle).
- If a sound disappears from the library, it was removed by the owner. Pick another.
